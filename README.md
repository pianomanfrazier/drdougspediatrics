# Dr. Doug's Pediatric Dentist Website

## Use Case

1. Make a payment
1. Find out more. Shopping and comparing dentists
1. What is the office like?
1. What is Dr. Doug like?
1. Location (Map, building, phone, address)

## Sections

- Payment with Snipcart
- Contact
- Address
- Office and Staff Pics
- FAQ & Resources
- Services & Procedures

## Tech Stack

- raw html
- feather icons
- alpine js for menu, accordions
- tachyons CSS
