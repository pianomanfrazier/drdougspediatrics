About our Office

Our office is designed for kids to have some fun and relax! Dr. Doug is easy going and has extra training in caring for infants, children and adolescents. You can see our staff members choose to work in a laid back pediatric dental office because they like pampering children and want to cater to their needs. These elements combine to make your child feel relaxed and special.

About Dr. Doug

Dr. K. Douglas Fryer, a.k.a. “Dr. Doug” was raised here in Utah. He attended Utah State University and is an avid Aggie basketball and track fan. He then attended The Ohio State University for dental school and became an avid Buckeye football fan. After dental school he finished a two year Pediatric Dentistry residency in order to specialize in working with children.

Dr. Doug is a Diplomate of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry. Dr. Doug lives in Logan with his wife and children. He says every day is a great day at work. He believes that kids do a great job in his office and that makes his job a success. He is thankful to work with great staff, sweet parents and Super Kids.
